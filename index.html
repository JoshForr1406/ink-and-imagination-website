Our story

About Ink & Imagination

Ink & Imagination – Forrester & Pickford Books brings together author Joshua Forrester and illustrator Steve Pickford. We want to create thoughtful, entertaining books and encourage children and aspiring creatives to share their own stories. From books with an important message to spooky adventures, we believe every imagination deserves a place.
